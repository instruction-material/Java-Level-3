# Reference reasoning

## Required policy
The first matching valid-request decision wins. A guest in a closed, busy lab receives guest:0 and only decision:guest. Validation precedes eligibility: missing audit wins over missing machine and invalid duration.

The blocked helper combines suspended/revoked with OR because either gives the same denial.
Readiness evaluates supervision first; reversing its OR operands adds an observable training probe.
Unsupervised STANDARD retains its training call even though every valid level passes.

Guard order matches the baseline. Excessive duration skips training/availability; insufficient training skips availability.
Each normal exit calls finish once. It appends to the caller's audit and grants zero minutes on denial.
The stable BookingClient signature/output and immutable event snapshots remain intact.

Enabled assertions reject the deliberate internal bad decision; disabled assertions let the demonstration observe it.
Required public validation and explicit contract checks remain active in both modes.

## Optional workflow
The factory selects a STANDARD, LASER or CNC implementation. STANDARD 30→4 and31→8; CNC 15→16 and16→23; LASER 60→126.
Selection remains conditional at the composition boundary while each variant owns its fee calculation and duration check.
The absent observer is normalized once. Its presence does not affect eligibility, credits or audit.
Approved LASER 60 reports started then completed:approved:126. Missing request reports started then failed:IllegalArgumentException:request and rethrows the same exception.
A missing audit remains an error. Guests remain denied with zero credits.
